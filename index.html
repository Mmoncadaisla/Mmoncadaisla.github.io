About me

I work at the intersection of software, data and environmental impact — that's where the problems I find most interesting tend to live. Most of my career has been about building useful things for small, technical teams: at Cambium I lead a small engineering team and span architecture, backend, cloud and geospatial; before that I spent almost four years at CARTO, where I grew into a support-lead role and also shipped end-to-end work for enterprise customers — FastAPI microservices, ETLs across PostgreSQL and BigQuery, and the Prefect/GCP-based data pipelines platform.

I like working with people. I think communication and being easy to work with matter as much as the code, and I genuinely enjoy the team side of the job. I help organise the PyData Madrid monthly meetups to stay close to the wider data community, and I collaborate with the GEOQUBIDY research group at UPM/URJC on cloud infrastructure for Earth observation work.

Outside work I'm a nature and mountain person. I travel a lot — mostly in our camper van with my partner and our dog Gilda — and I cook, write, and chip away at side projects around data and environmental causes when I can.
