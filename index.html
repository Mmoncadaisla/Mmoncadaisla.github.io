About me

I work at the intersection of software, data and environmental impact — that's where the problems I find most interesting tend to live. Most of my career has been about building useful things for small, technical teams: at Cambium I lead a small engineering team and span architecture, backend, cloud and geospatial; before that I spent almost four years at CARTO, where I grew into a support-lead role and also shipped end-to-end work for enterprise customers — FastAPI microservices, ETLs across PostgreSQL and BigQuery, and the Prefect/GCP-based data pipelines platform.

I like working with people. I think communication and being easy to work with matter as much as the code, and I genuinely enjoy the team side of the job. I help organise the PyData Madrid monthly meetups to stay close to the wider data community, and I collaborate with the GEOQUBIDY research group at UPM/URJC on cloud infrastructure for Earth observation work.

Outside work I'm a nature and mountain person. I travel a lot — mostly in our camper van with my partner and our dog Gilda — and I cook, write, and chip away at side projects around data and environmental causes when I can.

Where I've worked

The shorter version of my career — roughly chronological.

  1. Sep 2023 — present Madrid · Hybrid

    Technical Lead · Cambium

    I help shape the technical side of how we run nature-based carbon projects, from early evaluation through to monitoring and reporting. The team is small, so I'm involved in most of it — architecture, backend, geospatial, ML and a fair amount of hands-on coding.

    • Started data-canopy, the monorepo where most of our data, APIs and services live. It's where the team works day-to-day.
    • Currently building Canopy — the platform that brings the carbon team's tools into one place, shaped by feedback sessions with the people who actually use it.
    • Set up our data architecture on Google Cloud — Postgres/PostGIS on Kubernetes, Databricks for heavier compute, plus the usual Python data toolbox (Dask, Xarray, MLFlow).
    • Built the data integration pipeline for forestry partners — validated staging/production flow so quarter-end isn't a panic.
    • Designed and shipped Knowbase AI, an internal assistant that helps the team find answers in Verra documents, methodologies and research papers — with citations they can verify.
    • Trained an Above-Ground Biomass model aligned with Verra's VM0047 methodology, set up so the science team can re-run it on demand.
    • Built a site-selection tool for ARR projects in Brazil — most of the heavy lifting analysts used to do by hand.
    • Contributed to project design itself — protected-area analysis, species mix, spatial layout — trying to balance carbon goals with ecological ones.
  2. Aug 2022 — Sep 2023 CARTO

    Support Lead · CARTO

    Led the Support and Response teams — the people enterprise customers talk to when something is broken or urgent. Worked closely with Engineering, Sales and Customer Success to keep things moving and try to make customers feel heard.

    • Looked after a team of four engineers, and stepped in as Response Team Manager when the harder enterprise incidents came through.
    • Wired together a small system to give support agents the context they needed before replying, which helped us keep response times under SLA without people burning out.
    • Did Technical Account Management work for some of our larger customers — ETL, performance tuning, analytics on BigQuery / Redshift / Snowflake / Postgres.
    • Contributed code where I could — the Data Services API, CARTOframes, the Import API — usually starting from a customer-reported bug and working back to the cause.
